Academic Grade Appeal Protocol Overview

According to Chapter IV (Section 18) of the University Academic Handbook 2024, students may challenge a semester final grade under three legitimate conditions: calculation errors, rubric misapplication, or documented extenuating circumstances.

1
Informal Conference: Consult directly with the course instructor within 3 business days of grade release.
2
Formal Dossier Submission: If unresolved, lodge Form A-12 with your Academic Advisor endorsing the inquiry.
3
Faculty Board Review: The Vice Dean of Academic Affairs reviews the dossier; decisions issued within 10 days are final.
